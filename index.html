About MJ Cable Network

With over 20 years of rich experience in the media and entertainment industry, MJ Cable Network has grown from a humble local cable provider into a modern digital hub. Based proudly in Nellikkattiri, Palakkad district, we have spent the last two decades keeping our community informed, entertained, and connected. What started as a mission to deliver clear TV signals to local households has now evolved into a state-of-the-art network infrastructure. Today, we don't just bring you premium digital television through Kerala Vision Digital TV, but we also power local homes, businesses, and schools with high-speed fiber internet through India’s top broadband providers like Kerala Vision Broadband, BSNL Bharat Fiber, and KFON.
